Our story

A corner for coffee and long conversations क से कॉफ़ी

क Se Coffee sits by Metro Pillar No. 90, Wasudev Nagar Metro Station on Hingna Road. White arches, a draped canopy and strings of warm bulbs make it an easy place to sit a while.

The bar pulls proper espresso for cappuccinos and Spanish lattes, cold brews and matcha. The kitchen keeps it simple and filling: breakfast, pizza, burgers, pasta and Chinese, with a ₹249 meal combo when you just want lunch sorted.
